SEO

SEO is a long-term compounding strategy, not a quick fix. Here's what a realistic timeline looks like for most clients:

  • Month 1 to 2: Technical audit, fixes, on-page optimization foundation
  • Month 3 to 4: First ranking movements on target keywords
  • Month 5 to 8: Significant traffic growth, first page rankings emerging
  • Month 9 to 12: Compounding returns, substantial organic revenue contribution

We set honest expectations upfront. Anyone promising instant ranking magic is selling fairy dust in a blazer.

Our SEO service is comprehensive, not cookie-cutter. It includes technical SEO audit and remediation, keyword research and strategy, on-page optimization, content planning, schema markup, Core Web Vitals optimization, link building, and monthly performance reporting. The depth varies by plan tier, see our services page for a full breakdown.

Pricing & Contracts

We use two pricing models: monthly retainers for ongoing services like SEO and growth strategy , and fixed-price project quotes for web development, design, and branding. We never do hourly billing, it creates misaligned incentives. You always know exactly what you'll pay before we start.

None. Zero. The price in your proposal is the price you pay. The only additions would be if you request scope changes mid-project (which we handle through a clear change order process) or third-party tools/services you choose to add. We spell everything out upfront so there are never any surprises on your invoice.

For project-based work, we typically split payments: 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. For larger projects we can arrange milestone-based payments. Monthly retainers are billed at the start of each month. We accept bank transfer, bank transfer and major payment platforms.

For SEO retainers, we recommend a minimum 3-month engagement to see meaningful results, but we don't lock you into long contracts. After the initial 3 months, you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. Project-based work has no ongoing commitment. We earn your continued business through results, not contract clauses.

Working Together

Once you sign on, we kick off with a 60-minute discovery call to align on goals, timelines, and communication preferences. Then we run our audit/discovery phase (one to two weeks), present our strategy for your approval, and begin execution. You'll have a dedicated Notion workspace with your roadmap, assets, and progress tracking from day one.

We use Slack for day-to-day communication, Notion for project management and documentation, and scheduled video calls (bi-weekly or monthly, depending on your plan) for strategic reviews. You'll have direct access to your account manager, not a junior who escalates everything. Response time for messages: within 4 hours during business days.
